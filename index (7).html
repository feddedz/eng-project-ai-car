How the AI decides (every frame)

The loop runs 60 times a second.

flowchart TD
  A["New frame"] --> B{"Anything inside the viewing distance?"}
  B -- "No" --> C["Remember: road is clear"]
  B -- "Yes" --> D["Remember: what it is, how far, how fast"]
  C --> E["Wait the thinking time: act on what it saw that long ago"]
  D --> E
  E --> F{"Old view shows something close?"}
  F -- "No" --> G["Speed up to the speed limit"]
  F -- "Yes" --> H{"Is the next lane clear?"}
  H -- "Yes" --> I["Change lanes"]
  H -- "No" --> J["Slow down or stop"]
  G --> K["Move the car, check for crashes, update counters"]
  I --> K
  J --> K
  K --> A
